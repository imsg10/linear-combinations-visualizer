Think of v and w as two arrows. The scalar c stretches or shrinks v, and d stretches or shrinks w. Then you place them tip-to-tail.

Notice the dashed gray arrow: that's cv placed at the tip of dw. The green arrow is where you land — the combination.

As c and d range over all real numbers, what set of points does cv + dw reach?

Since v and w don't point in the same direction, they span the entire 2D plane — every point can be reached with the right c and d.
